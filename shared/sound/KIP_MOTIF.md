# Kip's motif ("voice")

Kip's signature sound, played whenever Kip does something important. Every Kettle Classics
game re-arranges it for its own sound chip, so Kip always sounds like Kip.

Original Kettle Forge Studios melody, 5 notes (frames at 60 per second):

| Note | G5 | C6 | E6 | D6 | G6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Length | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 14 |

Up a C-major chord, one step down, then a leap to the high G.

**POP jingle** (Kip pops): a short noise "pop", then C6 4, E6 4, G6 4, C7 18.

Used in: Kernel Drop (`pocket/kernel-drop/src/sound.asm`: `TuneKip`, `TunePop`).

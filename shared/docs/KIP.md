# Kip: style guide (draft)

Kip is a popcorn kernel: a small, round, golden kernel with a simple face.

- **Look:** rounded teardrop/kernel shape, two dot eyes, small smile. Keep it simple enough
  to read at 16x16 pixels on the Pocket, and scale up on bigger systems.
- **Colours:** golden yellow body with a darker hull edge (on Pocket: light grey body,
  dark outline).
- **Voice:** a short signature sound motif plus a "pop" jingle, re-arranged for each
  system's sound chip (Pocket pulse/wave/noise, 8-Bit, 16-Bit GEN FM, 16-Bit samples).
- **Story:** seed (Pocket) → field (Pocket Color) → harvest (8-Bit) → silo (16-Bit GEN)
  → kettle and POP (16-Bit). Bonus: caramel-corn sequel (Pocket 32).

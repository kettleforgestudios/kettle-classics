# Seed Room

Working title for an original Pocket Color planting puzzle. Alpha 1 has one room: pick up a seed, pull a leaf bridge into position, cross the ditch, and plant the seed in the soil on the other bank.

- D-pad moves Kip. A picks up the seed or plants it when standing on the plot.
- B pulls the leaf bridge from either bank on the crossing row. The ditch blocks movement until the bridge spans it.
- Select toggles hints. Start resets the room. A flower marks completion.

Build with RGBDS 1.0.4 and Python 3: `make -C pocket-color/seed-room`. The output is `seed-room.gbc` with a Pocket Color-only header and an empty logo region.
